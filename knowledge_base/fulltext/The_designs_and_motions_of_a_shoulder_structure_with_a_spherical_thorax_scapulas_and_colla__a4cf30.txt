The designs and Motions of a Shoulder Structure with a Spherical
Thorax, Scapulas and Collarbones for Humanoid “Kojiro”
Yoshinao Sodeyama, Tamaki Nishino, Yuta Namiki, Yuto Nakanishi, Ikuo Mizuuchi and Masayuki Inaba
Abstract— Humanoid robots are still in a process of the
evolution. We are trying to develop a next-generation which
have safety, ﬂexibility and multiplicity of uses. A Shoulder
structure is one of the keystone, because it controls arms and
end eﬀectors and it have direct contact with humans and daily
life environments. We have designed new shoulder structure
following the scapula-collarbone structure for a muscle-driven
humanoid robot named “Kojiro”(Fig. 1)[1]. We consider that
the scapula-collarbone structures have the following merits,
1)wide range of movement, 2)ﬂexible shoulder like humans,
3)safety from the driving system, 4)allowing space for a
lot of parts inside the thorax. The shoulder structure is
consisted of scapula, collarbone, upper-arm and thorax. We
have devised the spherical shape thorax and scapula structure
which realizes desired scapula motions. In this paper, we
describe the design process and operation test of the shoulder
structure.
I. INTRODUCTION
Recently, studies of humanoid robots make advances
to humans care in our daily life. In the future, it
will be usual in our daily life for humanoid robots to
assist humans directly. Then, humanoids must not be
dangerous to humans. Existing hardware of humanoid
robots are developed from the technology of industrial
machines which have hard bodies and signiﬁcant power
actuators, so it have a serious limitation about safety
for humans. It will be of the utmost necessity that
the humanoid robots have a safe and ﬂexible body
structures. The ﬂexibility of the arms and shoulders
will be especially important, because these parts will
come in direct contact with human bodies and daily life
environments.
In this light, we have developed a whole-type musculo-
skeletal humanoid Kojiro. Humanoid Kojiro is a substan-
tial improvement version of a humanoid “Kotaro” [2]. We
have mainly redesigned appropriate degrees of freedoms
of bone structures and optimal muscle arrangements for
the humanoids. In this paper, we explain new initiative
of the shoulder designs for Kojiro which has ﬂexibility
and wide range of movements.

collarbone structure include the following
1) Having eﬃcient muscle arrangements for wide
range of movement

3) Stabilization of scapulas posture

III. Spherical Shape Thorax and Scapula-Collarbone
Structure
A. Bone Parts Construction and Range of Movements
Developed shoudler structure is consisted of thorax,
scapula, collarbone, upper arm (Fig. 2). It is totally
5DOFs joint structure(Fig. 4).
1) Scapulohumeral joint (between scapula and upper
arm) : 3DOFs
2) Acromioclavicular joint (between scapula and col-
larbone) : 1DOF
3) Sternoclavicular joint (between collarbone and tho-
rax): 1DOF
Shoulder structure of Kotaro is consisted of 3DOFs
acromioclavicular joints and 3DOFs sternoclavicular
joints. Scapulas are constrained on chest surface, so the
shoulder is 6DOFs in total. Changing shoudler design to
Kojiro, it is stabilized in the rotational and translational
motions around thorax, but it can not move translational
motion in vertical direction. It can elevate arm (rotation
of scapula) and tracslational motion (scapula moving
forward and backward direction), so we consider that
it has enough range of movement for working.
Range of movements are shown in Fig. 5.
1) Acromioclavicular joint: initial attitude(Fig. 5 up-
side:red) +45 degrees(Fig. 5 upside:yellow) -45(Fig.
5 upside:green).
2) Sternoclavicular joint: initial attitube(Fig. 5 down-
side:red) +30 degrees(Fig. 5 downside:light blue)
-30 degrees(Fig. 5 downside:white).
Range of movement of the shoulder structure is ﬂexion
135 degrees to -60 degrees, abduction 60 degrees in total.

IV. THE DESIGN OF THE BODY WITH
SCAPULA-COLLARBONE STRUCTURES
The shoulder structure of Kojiro consists of four bone
parts which are the scapula, the collarbone, the upper
arm bone, and the chest. We introduce the design of
the bone structures. The parts are made of nylon 12
by Rapid Prototyping SLS (Selective Laser Sintering)
way [11]. The strength of the material is also equivalent
to ABS plastics ones. Each b one parts are designed as
the assembled structures of motors, boards, sensors, and
wires by using 3D-CAD.

A. The Design of the Spherical Thorax Parts
1) The Desige of a Spherical Shape and Parts Ar-
rangements: We have designed the thorax parts which
have spherical shape with radius 118mm (Fig. 9). Ster-
noclavicular joints and acromiclavicular joints of Kojiro
are 1DOF joint structures. The rotation axis of the
joints intersect at a central point of the spherical thorax
shape. The internal side shape of scapulas coming in
contact with the thorax is designed spherical shape with
radius 118mm same as the thorax shape. Accordingly,
the scapula can slide on the thorax surface with a certain
amount of friction and touch area. Sternoclavicular joints
and acromiclavicular joints have encoders at each axis, so
we can con trol arbitrary positions of the scapulas using
a 3D-geometrical model.
Thorax parts are designed that have AC-
motors(40Wx10), tension sensor(x12), motor driver
board(x5), USB-hub board(x2), and sensor board(x2)
inside it). The motor parts arrangements are desided
from the muscle arrangements. We selected the positions
of the parts which can have lower wire frictions and
large moment arms. The parts can be contained inside

the spherical shape, and we have considered ease of
maintenance.
Some actuators and boards for driving Kojiro neck and
head have been designed as the unitizing parts under the
neck. The thorax parts can con tain the neck unitizing
parts inside the body. It realizes ease of maintenance of
the head and neck unit. The thorax has the spine ball-
and-socket joint at the bottom. It has mounting points
of rubber and springs for connecting to the spine parts.
2) Interface of the lines for the whole body: The back
side of the thorax has the input interface of the power
lines and USB logic line from a outside power supply and
computer for Kojiro whole body. The thorax contains the
multidrop lines to the head, arms, spines, pelvis, and legs.
It has the enough space for the lines and connectors. The
base power lines and USB line are held on the thorax
bone to prevent the tension for each connectors.
3) Heat countermeasure for high temperatures: An
AC40W-motor rise in temperature to 90 degrees when
it is in high output. The thorax contains 10 AC40W-
motors contiguously inside it, and it closes up by the
scapulas around the thorax surface, so the heat of the
motors is especially problem for the thorax. The thorax
has 4 cooling fans(40x40mm) and ﬂow path. Hot sections
of the motors and boards have been designed on the ﬂow
path.

B. The Design of Collarbone Parts
We designed the collarbones made of A7075 (Fig.
12:left). Both ends of the collarbone have bearings and
encoders. It has an arch shape to avoid interpositions
with neck parts and thorax parts.
Collarbones are the only structural links between a
thorax and scapulas, so the forces to the arms act
directly to the parts. At the human body, many strong
muscles (for example, preater pectoral muscle, anterior
serratus muscle and latissimus dorsi muscle) connect
scapulas to a thorax. Humanoid Kojiro have four muscles
between a scapula and a thorax, so it needs strength
design for collarbones. We have designed the collarbones
which can take the maximal load 300[N] at the point
of acromioclavicular joint. A length between the center
point of an acromioclavicular joint and a sternoclavicular
joint is 85[mm], so the maximal moment load is 29.8[Nm]
to a sternoclavicular joint. A cross-sectional shape of
collarbones is the minimum diameter 11[mm]. The max
stress is 32[MN/m2] and allowable buckiling stress is
205[[MN/m2], so factor of safety is 6.3.It is suﬃcient in
intensity.
C. The Design of Scapula Parts
A bladebone parts are designed that can con tain
40W-AC motors(x2), 4.5W-DC motors(x3), tension sen-
sors(x5), motor driver boards(x2), sensor boards(x1),
USB-hub board(x1) (Fig. 10). Scapulas can rotate and
anterior-posterior slide motion on the spherical thorax.
It realizes wide range of movements of the shoulder joint,
but the friction is top issue of the scapula-collabone

Thorax side surfaces of the scapulas are always on a
spherical thorax. Then, the scapulas curved surface is

static friction is 0.02, coeﬃcient of dynamic friction is
0.03). It reduces the friction between scapulas and a
chest. This PTFE parts is one of the breakthrough of

D. Parts Arrangements for Elevation of the Upper Arms
The shape of scapula is designed which have large
moment arms of muscles and can con tain a lot of parts.
At the design of humanoid Kotaro, main actuators for
elevation of the upper arms were contained in the chest
parts. We used corrugated tubes from the chest to
bladebones for the muscles. It was diﬃcult to generate
an elevate arm motion by the frictions of the structure.
When we try to develop the shoulder which can elevate
an arm motion using a musculo-skeletal body structure,
the top issue is diﬃculty of design the body which have
large moment arms from the upper part of the body.
The human shoulder can not obtains comparatively
large moment arms. Human muscle is stronger than
humanoids one, so it can ease to elevate arm motion.
Nevertheless a human shoulder has lots of troubles (just
like stiﬀ shoulder and frozen shoulder), so the shoulder
is diﬃcult to drive for a human body. At the design of
Kojiro, actuators lie directly on the upper arm bone. It
reduces the friction caused by corrugated tubes. We have
designed a apophysis which have 40W-motor to obtain
large moment arms on foreside of the scapula.
E. The Design of Upper Arms Parts
An Upper arm parts are designed that have 40W-
AC-motors(x2), tension sensors(x2), a motor driver
board(x1), a sensor board(x1), and a USB hub
board(x1)(Fig. 13). The muscles driving a ﬂexion move-
ment of an elbow has large moment arm, which start
70[mm] upper point from a center of the elbow joint.
Additionally, foreside of the upper arm parts have been
designed ﬁve attachment points of muscles from scapulas.

VI. Conclutions and Future Works
We have indicated the designs and motions of a new
shoulder structure for Kojiro. A scapula can move in
very few frictions between a scapula and a thorax as
planned. It can generate motions which have wide range
of movement and ﬂexibility.
After this, we will provide a reaching motion, a crank
revolving motion, using this shoulder structure with
whole body of Kojiro. Additionally, we will study about
human care actions having direct contact with humans.
